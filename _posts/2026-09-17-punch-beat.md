The first full song played by the prototype, recorded straight off its analog output.

It started two days ago as a one-bar loop to hear the compressors work: a boom bap kit, a keys loop and an electric bass, with the kick ducking both. It grew into six sections chained together and played by the instrument's own sequencer, from intro to outro, in one take.

The levels, the sidechain pump, the parallel compression and the limiter on the master are all the device. Off the device, the recording only got a trim, a fade, a little EQ and loudness for streaming.
