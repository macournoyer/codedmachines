What we’re building

01

Hands on everything

Play it live, or program it step by step. Every sound and every move is one gesture away, with no menus in the way of the music.

02

Built for the stage

Dedicated audio processors do the sound while a separate computer runs the interface, so playback never stutters, whatever you throw at it.

03

Designed in-house

Enclosure, circuit boards, firmware and software, all developed by us from the ground up, and tested on the bench every day.

Company

Coded Machines is an independent maker of electronic musical instruments, founded by musician and software engineer Marc-André Cournoyer.

We make standalone hardware for sampling, sequencing and live performance, instruments meant to be played with both hands. Our first product is in active development, and this log follows it week by week.
