Five weeks working on the sampler. It's hard to stop! I think I'm on to something :).

Early this week I spent some time hardening the hell out of it, and the result is solid. I was able to make and record the first full song without _any_ crashes or glitches.

Last week I spent a lot of time building a full mastering chain with sidechain ducking on the device, and I think it's paying off. The sound, out of the box, is great. And because it has a web API, I can ask Claude to master it for me (which I did here).

Although this demo barely scratches the surface, I'm very excited and proud of how easy it is to make something that sounds great, quickly.

A lot more awesome stuff coming soon!
