Company

Coded Machines is an independent maker of electronic musical instruments, founded by musician and software engineer Marc-André Cournoyer.

We make standalone hardware for sampling, sequencing and live performance, instruments meant to be played with both hands. Our first product is in active development, with more to share soon.
